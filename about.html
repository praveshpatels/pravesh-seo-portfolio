About Me

SEO strategy backed by data, technical understanding, and execution.

I'm Pravesh Patel, an SEO professional, author, and blogger based in Ahmedabad, India, with 9+ years of experience helping businesses improve organic visibility, search performance, and online discoverability.

My approach to SEO

I approach SEO as a long-term growth system—not a collection of isolated tactics. I use search data and technical analysis to identify opportunities, prioritize implementation, and measure the impact of changes.

My work spans technical SEO, site architecture, internal linking, keyword and search-intent research, content optimization, CTR improvement, and link-profile analysis. I aim to build useful, well-structured websites that serve searchers and follow Google Search Central guidance.

Areas I work across

  • Technical SEO, crawlability, indexation, and site health
  • On-page SEO, metadata, internal linking, and content structure
  • Keyword research, competitor analysis, and search-intent mapping
  • Programmatic SEO and scalable location/category page structures
  • Content marketing, topical coverage, and blog strategy
  • Backlink audits, link building, and authority growth
  • Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
  • SEO reporting, opportunity analysis, and team coordination

A repeatable process helps keep SEO work focused, collaborative, and measurable.

01

Audit and diagnose

Understand the site's technical condition, search visibility, content, competitors, and opportunities before recommending changes.

02

Prioritize and implement

Build an actionable roadmap, coordinate with writers and developers where needed, and focus effort on work with a clear rationale.

03

Measure and refine

Review Search Console, analytics, rankings, and technical indicators to understand what changed and where to improve next.
