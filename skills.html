Core SEO capabilities

Areas of practice relevant to SEO Specialist, Senior SEO Specialist, Technical SEO, and SEO Manager roles.

01 · Technical SEO

  • Crawlability, indexation, and site-health analysis
  • XML sitemaps, robots.txt, canonical tags, and redirects
  • 404/410 status handling and URL cleanup
  • Site architecture, internal linking, and crawl-path improvements
  • Structured data and schema implementation planning
  • Core Web Vitals and technical issue prioritization
  • Website migration checks and post-launch monitoring

02 · On-page & content SEO

  • Keyword research and competitor analysis
  • Search intent, SERP analysis, and content-gap identification
  • Title tags, meta descriptions, headings, and page-level optimization
  • Content briefs, refreshes, and quality improvements
  • Topical coverage and internal-link planning
  • CTR analysis and ranking-opportunity prioritization

03 · Programmatic & local SEO

  • Scalable category, location, and landing-page architecture
  • Page-template and metadata planning for large URL sets
  • Internal linking across related locations and categories
  • Local intent and “near me” search optimization concepts
  • Quality checks to reduce thin, repetitive, or low-value pages

04 · Off-page SEO & link profiles

  • Backlink-profile and referring-domain analysis
  • Lost-link and broken-backlink investigation
  • Link prospect assessment for relevance and quality
  • Editorial and contextual link-building planning
  • Competitor backlink research and opportunity prioritization

05 · Analytics & reporting

  • Google Search Console clicks, impressions, CTR, and position analysis
  • Google Analytics performance review and traffic segmentation
  • Keyword visibility and competitor trend monitoring
  • Opportunity analysis, KPI reporting, and performance summaries
  • SEO dashboards and spreadsheet-based analysis
  • Separating first-party analytics from third-party traffic estimates

06 · Search evolution & discoverability

  • Helpful, intent-aligned content planning
  • Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) concepts
  • Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) concepts
  • Structured, clear content that supports search understanding
  • Monitoring emerging search surfaces without losing focus on user value
